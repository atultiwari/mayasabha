Room Map · start here

Fit the picture to your wall and trace your frames, shelf, door and puja picture once. Every wall activity then uses them.

Room Pet

A puppy that lives on the wall, eats fruit, jumps when you clap and plays with the elephant, monkey and mouse.

Ceiling Window

A hole opens in the ceiling: sky, aquarium or stars, with friends peeking down. Bedtime mode fades to black.

Bubbles & Butterflies

Pop giant bubbles by touching the wall (some hide a surprise). Hold a hand still and butterflies land on it. Uses a camera.

Antariksh अंतरिक्ष

The real solar system on your ceiling: fly to any planet and hear about it in English or Hindi, compare true sizes, take a guided tour.

Rocket Ride to Chanda Mama

A bedtime story where your child is the hero: clap to blast off, jump on the Moon, wave away Mars dust, pop Jupiter's storms… English or Hindi.

Maya Masks

Real projection mapping: trace your frames, door, balloons and floor, then light them up. Diwali, Magic, Party.

Cushion Physics

Real cushions become solid: fruit bounces off them into Hathi's trunk. Move a cushion to change the path. Marbles play music.

Kona कोना

A 3D illusion on a room corner: the walls break open into space, a cube floats out, fish swim behind glass.
